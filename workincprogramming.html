My Works in C Programming Course

During the last semester, I completed various assignments, projects, and online challenges in C Programming. Below are the details of my achievements.

HackerRank - Work, Certificates and Badges

I dedicated significant time to practicing on HackerRank to strengthen my C programming skills. I solved numerous problems covering topics such as basic syntax, conditionals, loops, arrays, pointers, functions, and string manipulation. Starting with easy challenges like "Hello World in C", "Sum and Difference of Two Numbers", and "Playing With Characters", I gradually progressed to more difficult ones involving pointers and dynamic memory.

The platform helped me understand common mistakes and improve code efficiency. After consistent practice, I successfully earned the HackerRank C (Basic) Certificate. I also received several badges for problem-solving consistency and accuracy. This experience greatly improved my debugging skills and logical thinking. Practicing on HackerRank complemented the classroom lessons perfectly and prepared me well for examinations. I am proud of the progress I made through daily practice and determination.

SoloLearn - Work and Certificates

I also used SoloLearn as a supplementary learning tool for C programming. The interactive lessons made learning engaging and flexible. I completed the entire C course, which included modules on basic concepts, control structures, functions, arrays, pointers, structures, and memory management.

SoloLearn’s quiz system and code coach feature allowed me to test my knowledge immediately after each lesson. I enjoyed solving daily challenges and competing on the leaderboard. After finishing all modules and passing the final test, I received the official SoloLearn C Programming Certificate. This platform was especially helpful for revision and learning at my own pace. Combining SoloLearn with university lectures and HackerRank gave me a well-rounded understanding of the C language.
